Suggested order

Move through layers — traits → type → motivations → symbolic identity.
1
Big Five — trait foundation OpenPsychometrics or BigFive-Test. Map your core trait profile first.
2
MBTI-style — type pattern 16Personalities or Truity TypeFinder. Get a recognisable type snapshot.
3
Enneagram — motivations & defenses Truity Enneagram. Reveals what drives you and what you protect against.
4
Archetype — symbolic identity layer Pick one archetype test. The most subjective layer — but often the most striking.
